# G-protein receptors
G-protein receptors (GPCRs) are ligand gated receptors that transmit signals from the outside via the cAMP pathway or the phosphatidylinositol pathway. GPCRs are one the most common drug targets. GPCRs signaling can increase or decrease excitability of neurons or change release probability or neurotransmitters. Chemogenetics is the use of GPCRs to modify the function of neurons.

## Muscarinic acetylcholine receptors
There are five muscarinic acetylcholine receptors (mAChRs). $M_1$, $M_3$, and $M_5$ are Gq coupled. $M_2$ and $M_4$ are Gi coupled. mAChRs are sensitive to muscarine. mAChR agonism/antagonism often have pretty undesirable side effects. They are highly expressed in the periphery making them hard to use a CNS drug targets.
